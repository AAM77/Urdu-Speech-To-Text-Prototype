# Transcription Prompt — English Audio to American English (v1)

Please analyze the attached **English-language** audio clip and transcribe it as a
standalone **American English** text. Assume this is **one chunk** in a sequence
of audio segments. Preserve meaning faithfully; treat the transcript as prose
worthy of publication. There might be some Urdu in there, so translate it.

Apply the following strict guidelines to your final output:

**Format and tone:** Produce a word-for-word transcript. Remove filler sounds,
false starts, and needless verbal tics unless they carry meaning (e.g.,
hesitation emphasized for rhetoric). Structure the output as cohesive,
standalone prose with a short descriptive **title** and logical **paragraph
breaks**.

**Regional variety:** Normalize spelling and vocabulary toward **United States
American English** (e.g., *color*, *recognize*, *behavior* where UK speakers
might use other variants). Keep proper nouns, quoted titles, and explicit
mentions of alternate spellings as spoken.

**Clarity:** Use standard American punctuation and paragraphing. Preserve
digits, technical terms, and loanwords unless the speaker clearly translates or
defines them differently.

**Religious / Arabic terminology:** Follow the same glossary spirit as elsewhere
in this project — when Arabic or Islamic terminology appears, you may italicize a
consistent lightweight transliteration in parentheses following the customary
American English context (matching the project's translation norms where
applicable). Do not overload the transcript with footnotes.

**Faithfulness:** Do **not** invent sentences or doctrines. For audio that is
genuinely unclear or inaudible, mark that span with `[unclear]` instead of guessing.

**Context block:** If you are given a "previous chunk tail" block below, use it only
for continuity (names, dangling sentences). **Do not** transcribe words from it;
transcribe only the attached audio chunk.
